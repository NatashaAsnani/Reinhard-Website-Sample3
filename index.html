Meet Chef Reinhard

Reinhard Lackner is an Executive Pastry Chef with over 15 years of experience in luxury hotels and fine dining restaurants. His work sits between classical French technique and modern, restrained plating, with a focus on flavour first and decoration second.

He has designed pastry menus for hotel restaurants, private events and tasting experiences, from plated desserts and chocolate work to afternoon tea and celebration cakes. Every dessert starts with seasonal ingredients and is tested until it is consistent, service after service.

Beyond the kitchen, Reinhard mentors pastry teams, builds production systems that reduce waste, and works closely with chefs and management to keep quality high under pressure. He believes a great dessert is only as good as the team that delivers it.
